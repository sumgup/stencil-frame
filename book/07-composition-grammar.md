# Composition Grammar

> Composition is standpoint.
>
> What the founder concludes is decided by where you made them stand.

---

# Purpose

Composition Grammar defines how evidence is arranged so that understanding belongs to the founder rather than the system.

If Motion Grammar governs time, and Gesture Grammar governs authority, Composition Grammar governs position.

It asks:

Where must the founder stand for the material to argue for itself?

---

# Governing Question

Where must the founder stand for the material to argue for itself?

---

# First Principles

Composition is not arranging objects.

It is constructing the vantage point from which a conclusion becomes unavoidable.

The conclusion is never placed on the page.

The founder reaches it.

---

# Eye Journey

Look → Read → Recognize → Return → Understand

Return is the defining beat.

Every composition should reward a second look.

---

# Core Laws

## One Conclusion

Many marks. One argument.

## Structural Surprise

Surprise the founder with the material, never with the design.

Priority:

Structural

Conceptual

Emotional

Visual

## White Space

White space is withheld judgment.

It is not breathing room.

## Margins

The margin is where Stencil may speak.

The centre belongs to the founder.

## Alignment

Precision belongs to the substrate.

Discovery belongs to the occupation of that substrate.

## Hierarchy

Never by scale or force.

Always by position, isolation, sequence and convergence.

## Hidden Information

Nothing is hidden.

Everything is present.

The table is large.

Distance reveals.

## Asymmetry

Working surfaces are asymmetrical.

Reference surfaces may resolve.

---

# Structural Invariants

Composition governs relationships rather than objects.

The same law repeats from page to product:

One conclusion.

One second look.

Many marks.

---

# Design Consequences

Stop composing for the first look.

Start composing for the second.

Stop making things bigger.

Start making relationships clearer.

---

# Counterexamples

Apple-style centered hero

Generic SaaS feature grids

Dribbble-first layouts

Pinterest moodboards

Bento grids as hierarchy

---

# Misinterpretations

Composition is not layout.

Composition is not balance.

Composition is not density.

Composition is the arrangement of evidence into an argument.

---

# Design Review

A composition is ready when the founder can explain its conclusion but cannot point to the single element that produced it.

Composition is not concerned with where objects sit.
