# Stencil

> A discipline for making decisions that can be checked later.

---

# Purpose

Stencil is a discipline for making decisions that can be checked later.

It is not software.

It is not AI.

It is not a product.

Products may implement it.

Software may host it.

AI may assist it.

None of these define it.

---

# The Single Requirement

Every grammar exists to enforce one requirement.

> **A conclusion is only worth having if it can be checked—and it can only be checked if the record of how it was reached survives intact.**

Every chapter applies this requirement to a different medium.

| Grammar | Protects checkability in |
|----------|--------------------------|
| Motion | Time |
| Gesture | Contact |
| Composition | Adjacency |
| Spatial | Standing |
| Visual | Appearance |
| Material | Substance |
| Interaction | Authority |
| Diagnosis | Absence |
| Brand | Purpose |
| Scene | Testimony |

---

# Law One

> **Nothing in Stencil may produce a conclusion the founder cannot check.**

Every other law derives from this one.

---

# Permanent Provisions

The following are constitutional.

They are not amended.

They define Stencil itself.

## 1

The founder authors the centre.

Stencil may object.

It may never write the answer.

---

## 2

Nothing is removed.

Rejected work remains legible.

The only permitted disappearance is Stencil withdrawing its own uncommitted proposal.

---

## 3

Nothing may claim a history it does not have.

---

## 4

The founder decides.

Refusal requires no justification.

Not even to Stencil.

---

# What May Change

Everything except the permanent provisions.

Including:

- implementation
- interface
- motion
- typography
- tooling
- repository structure
- diagnostics
- materials
- products

Vocabulary is never sacred.

Behaviour is.

---

# Supersession

Laws are never edited.

They are superseded.

A supersession requires evidence from a real founder session demonstrating that a law prevented a founder from reaching a conclusion they could defend.

Every supersession records:

- the contradiction
- the original law
- the replacement law
- supporting evidence
- downstream consequences

The previous law remains visible.

History is preserved.

---

# Authority

Only the constitutional author may supersede a law.

Others may propose.

Nobody may silently rewrite.

The constitution obeys the same rules it places upon the product.

---

# Closing

> Every mark is an argument someone was willing to lose something for.

Everything in Stencil exists so that argument survives the person who made it.
