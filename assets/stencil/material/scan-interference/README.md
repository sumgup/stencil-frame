# scan-interference

**Status:** AWAITING PRODUCTION — no assets yet.

## What belongs here

Textures documenting scanning artefacts: moiré, scanning artefacts,
reproduction interference.

## Why this material exists

Scan interference implies **imperfect transmission** — the act of digitizing
a physical thing leaves a signature of its own, distinct from the original.

## Rules

- Production assets only after they are created/photographed/scanned by us, or
  explicitly licensed for our use. See `assets/stencil/ASSET-MANIFEST.md`.
- Do not add placeholder or fabricated textures. If nothing exists yet, this
  README is the only file in the folder.
- Every asset added here must be logged in the manifest's provenance table.
