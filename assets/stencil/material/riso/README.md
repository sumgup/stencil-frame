# riso

**Status:** AWAITING PRODUCTION — no assets yet.

## What belongs here

Textures and captures documenting risograph printing: registration offset,
ink overlap, imperfect reproduction, colour separation.

## Why this material exists

Riso printing implies **reproduction under mechanical imprecision** — layers
of ink laid down separately, never perfectly aligned. The asset should carry
that misalignment as evidence, not hide it.
